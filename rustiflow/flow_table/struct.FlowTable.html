Processes a packet (either IPv4 or IPv6) and updates the flow map.

Both directions of a biflow share one canonical map entry, so each packet costs a single hash-table lookup.

fn apply_packet_to_flow( entry: &mut FlowEntry<T>, packet: &PacketFeatures, is_forward: bool, early_export_us: Option<i64>, ) -> FlowUpdate<T>

Applies a packet to a flow and decides whether it terminated the flow or reached the flow’s next early-export boundary.

Early exports are cumulative snapshots of the flow so far, closed like a final export but with cause None. Only packets trigger them, so a flow that goes quiet gets no further snapshots until it expires.
